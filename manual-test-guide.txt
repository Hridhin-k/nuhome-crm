NUHOME CRM — MANUAL TEST GUIDE
================================
For testers. Follow every numbered step. Tick the Expected result after each step.
Do not skip steps. Use a fresh incognito window when switching roles unless told otherwise.


1. HOW TO START
---------------
App URL (local):     http://localhost:3000
Login URL:           http://localhost:3000/login

If you cannot log in, ask a developer to seed demo users:
  npx supabase db query --linked -f supabase/seed.sql

Use TWO browsers (or one normal + one incognito) when a flow says "keep Sales logged in".
That is required for live-update (Realtime) checks.


2. DEMO CREDENTIALS (every role)
--------------------------------
Password for EVERY account:  password123

Email                      Role in system     Name shown in app     Bottom-nav label
-------------------------  -----------------  --------------------  -----------------
sales@nuhome.demo          sales              Sales Demo            Sales
accounts@nuhome.demo       accounts           Accounts Demo         Accounts
procurement@nuhome.demo    procurement        Procurement Demo      Procurement
store@nuhome.demo          store              Store Demo            Delivery
admin@nuhome.demo          admin              Admin Demo            Admin

IMPORTANT
- The Store login appears in the app as "Delivery". That is expected.
- Admin can do every action. Prefer the dedicated role for realistic testing.
- Sales cannot approve quotes or verify payments.
- Accounts cannot record payments or send quotes to customers.
- The person who created a quote cannot approve that quote (even as Admin if they created it).
- The person who recorded a payment cannot verify that payment.


3. HOW TO SIGN IN / SIGN OUT
----------------------------
Sign in
  1. Open http://localhost:3000/login
  2. Enter Email from the table above.
  3. Enter Password: password123
  4. Tap Sign in.
  Expected: You land on Home. Navbar shows your name and role.

Sign out (required when switching users in the SAME browser)
  1. Tap More (bottom nav or desktop nav).
  2. Tap the Account card (name / email / role).
  3. Tap Sign out.
  Expected: You return to the login screen.

Wrong password
  1. On /login enter sales@nuhome.demo and password "wrongpass".
  2. Tap Sign in.
  Expected: Error message. You stay on login.

Empty fields
  1. Leave email or password blank and tap Sign in.
  Expected: Browser required-field validation. No login.


4. WHAT EACH ROLE SHOULD SEE IN THE NAV
---------------------------------------
After login, confirm the nav items (desktop top + mobile bottom).

Sales
  Home | Customers | Quotes | Orders | More
  Home also shows Quick Actions: "Customer walk-in" and "Find customer".

Accounts
  Home | Approvals | Payments | Orders | More
  Home queues: Quotes awaiting approval, Payments awaiting verification, Orders requiring attention.

Procurement
  Home | Orders | Vendors | Fulfillment | More
  Home queues: Awaiting vendor action, Vendor dispatches, Items expected.

Store (shown as Delivery)
  Home | Ready | Orders | More
  Home queues: Ready for delivery, Blocked by payment.

Admin
  Home | Users | Orders | Reports | More
  Home shows Overview metrics and a pipeline of all stages.

Role-gate check (do this once per role)
  1. While logged in as Sales, type http://localhost:3000/approvals in the address bar.
     Expected: Redirected to /home (Sales cannot approve).
  2. Type http://localhost:3000/payments
     Expected: Redirected to /home.
  3. Type http://localhost:3000/fulfillment
     Expected: Redirected to /home.
  4. Type http://localhost:3000/ready
     Expected: Redirected to /home.
  5. Type http://localhost:3000/users
     Expected: Redirected to /home.

     Expected: Redirected to /home (Accounts cannot create quotes).
  7. Log in as Store. Type http://localhost:3000/vendors
